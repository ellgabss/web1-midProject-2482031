My Holiday

This holiday was a very memorable one. Besides taking a break from my studies, I spent three months selling health and spiritual books. The trip started with two weeks in Tangerang, one week in Temanggung, and two months in Bali. In Bali, we offered English books directly to foreign tourists, which made the experience more challenging and meaningful. Travelling to different places and meeting buyers from many countries taught me a lot about courage, communication, and independence.
